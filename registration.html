Registration

Registrations are already open! They close 30 September 2026 for contractual reasons.

The earlier you register, the easier it is for the organizers, so please do not hesitate.

The registration fee is around €750. It includes

  • 5 nights accommodation at the Villa Clythia with breakfast, lunch, and dinner,
  • Tea, coffee, and snacks, and
  • Tourist tax, administration fees, and value-added tax (VAT).

The conference facilities are kindly sponsored by the Inria center at Université Grenoble Alpes.
